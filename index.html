How to play

  • Every tile is an 8-bit number (shown as binary + decimal).
  • Swiping slides tiles; when two collide they XOR:
    0010 ^ 0100 = 0110 — different bits combine.
  • Equal tiles cancel: 0011 ^ 0011 = 0000 — your "delete" tool.
  • Overlapping bits are lost: 0110 ^ 0011 = 0101 — wasted potential.
  • Shift tool. Every 50 points earns a << charge. Spend one to double a tile: 01000000 becomes 10000000. XOR never carries, so this is the only way to make bit 7 yourself instead of waiting for a 128 to spawn.
  • Goal: forge the full register 11111111 = 255.
  • Score = total bits set on merges. No lose state — it's a puzzle, take your time.
  • Orders. The bar shows a byte. Forge that byte.
  • An example. The order is 00010100. It asks for two bits — bit 4 and bit 2. Bit 4 on its own is the tile 16. Bit 2 on its own is the tile 4. Merge those two and you have 00010100, which is 20. The order is filled.
  • The tile then comes off the board, and another byte is asked for.
  • Two tiles arrive each move here, not one. The board stays busier, so you are picking between merges instead of waiting for a bit to turn up.
  • Watch the overlap. A tile holding a bit the order does not ask for makes the byte wrong. Two tiles holding the same bit cancel it to 0. Look for tiles whose bits do not overlap.
  • That goes on for as long as you want. There is no timer, and you cannot lose. Press New when you have had enough.
  • An order never asks for fewer than four bits, because a spawn would fill it for you.
  • Most orders counts orders filled, not points. Each mode keeps its own board and its own record.
